1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVES
================================================================================
Ang project na ito ay isang Web-Based 3D Traffic Microsimulation at Decision 
Support System (DSS) na ginawa para suriin, ma-simulate, at ma-visualize ang 
daloy ng trapiko sa sentro ng Kabankalan City, partikular sa intersection ng 
Guanzon Street at Bacolod-South Road na katabi ng Ceres Bus Terminal.

PANGUNAHING LAYUNIN:
1. Magkaroon ng accurate 3D Digital Twin ng corridor alinsunod sa DPWH at 
   AASHTO Geometric Design Standards.
2. I-simulate ang halo-halong trapiko sa Pilipinas (Paratransit: Ceres Buses, 
   Jeepneys, Tricycles, Private Cars, at Pedestrians).
3. Magbigay ng siyentipikong datos para sa mga Civil Engineers at Planners 
   gamit ang Highway Capacity Manual (HCM) Level of Service (LOS) at Surrogate 
   Safety Assessment Model (SSAM) para sa safety conflicts (TTC at PET).

2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & HOW IT WAS BUILT (KUNG PAANO GINAWA)
================================================================================

2.1 TECHNOLOGY STACK
--------------------
- Frontend Framework: Next.js 15 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript
  -> Pinili ito para sa maximum type safety, mabilis na rendering, at modular 
     component architecture.
- 3D Graphics Engine: Three.js via @react-three/fiber at @react-three/drei
  -> Hardware-accelerated WebGL rendering para makapag-render ng daan-daang 
     moving vehicles, pedestrians, at 3D environment sa browser nang walang lag 
     (60 FPS).
- Custom Physics & Simulation Engine:
  -> Mathematical engine gamit ang custom requestAnimationFrame loop na may 
     variable delta-time (dt) at sub-stepping para maiwasan ang frame drops at 
     physics clipping.
- UI & Data Visualization: Tailwind CSS, Lucide React, at HTML5 Canvas overlays.


2.2 MATHEMATICAL NODE-SEGMENT ROAD NETWORK (networkGraph.ts & CorridorLayout.tsx)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hindi gumamit ng simpleng 3D boxes o manual placement. Ang buong road network 
ay ibinase sa simcity-style graph theory:
- Nodes (Junctions & Waypoints): 
  Bawat kritikal na lokasyon ay may exact 3D coordinate (X, Y, Z).
  * Node 1: Guanzon St / Bacolod-South Road North
  * Node 2: Guanzon St / Pedro Yulo Intersection
  * Node 3: Ceres Terminal Ingress (Gate 1)
  * Node 4: Ceres Terminal Egress (Gate 2)
  * Node 5: KCC Mall Frontage
  * Node 6: Public Market & Transport Hub
  * Node 7: Bacolod-South Road Southbound Exit
- Procedural Ribbons & Fillets:
  * Ang kalsada ay extruded ribbons na may 0.15m raised beveled concrete curbs.
  * DPWH-compliant lane markings: crisp dashed white lane dividers, solid 
    yellow centerlines, at textured pedestrian crosswalks.
  * Walang z-fighting o overlapping meshes dahil bawat junction ay may 
    fillet/corner apron geometry.


2.3 VEHICLE BEHAVIOR & INTELLIGENT DRIVER MODEL (IDM) (vehicles.ts)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Para maging makatotohanan ang galaw ng mga sasakyan, ipinatupad ang Intelligent 
Driver Model (IDM) na standard sa transport engineering:
- Equation:
  dv/dt = a * [ 1 - (v / v0)^delta - (s* / s)^2 ]
  Kung saan:
  * v0  = Desired free-flow speed (30 - 45 km/h)
  * s0  = Minimum jam distance (2.0m para sa kotse, 3.5m para sa Ceres bus)
  * T   = Safe time headway (1.2s - 1.8s)
  * a   = Maximum acceleration (1.2 - 2.5 m/s²)
  * b   = Comfortable deceleration (1.5 - 2.8 m/s²)
- Anti-Clipping & Physical Collision Prevention:
  * May 3D bounding box ang bawat sasakyan (Bus: 12.0m x 2.5m; Jeep: 6.5m x 1.8m; 
    Tricycle: 2.2m x 1.4m).
  * Na-resolve ang "ghost-walking" bug sa pamamagitan ng forward collision cone at 
    hard-clamp logic kapag ang distance sa sinusundang sasakyan ay bumaba sa safe gap.


2.4 PEDESTRIAN DYNAMICS & SOCIAL FORCE MODEL (pedestrians.ts)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Curb Waiting & Gap Acceptance:
  * Ang mga pedestrians ay HINDI basta tumatawid sa kalsada.
  * Bago tumapak sa aspalto, kinakalkula ang Time-to-Collision (TTC) ng paparating 
    na sasakyan. Kung TTC < 3.0 seconds, maghihintay ang pedestrian sa bangketa.
- Aggressive Bus, Defensive Pedestrian Dynamic:
  * Ang mga Ceres Bus ay may priority aggression factor.
  * Kung papalapit ang malaking bus habang tumatawid ang pedestrian, ang pedestrian 
    ay bibilisan ang paglakad o hihinto upang magbigay-daan (yielding behavior).


2.5 CERES BUS TERMINAL REALISTIC LOGISTICS (routes.ts & vehicles.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Inayos ang simulation para maiwasan ang biglaang paglitaw (spawning out of nowhere) 
o pagtagos sa bakod:
1. Ingress (Gate 1): Papasok ang bus galing sa highway at babagal papasok sa driveway.
2. Alighting Platform: Hihinto ang bus sa designated unloading zone para magbaba ng 
   pasahero (dwell time: 10-25 seconds).
3. Staging / Queuing Bay: Lilipat ang bus sa parking/staging bays at pipila nang 
   maayos na may pisikal na distansya sa ibang bus.
4. Egress (Gate 2): Lalabas lamang ang bus kapag may clearance sa highway at 
   kakanan o kakaliwa nang may wide-turning radius.
5. Scheduled Dispatching: Na-adjust ang bus spawning upang sumunod sa hourly rate 
   (e.g., 1-3 bus arrivals/departures kada simulation cycle), na akma sa totoong 
   iskedyul ng Ceres Liner.


2.6 SURROGATE SAFETY ASSESSMENT MODEL (SSAM) (conflicts.ts)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Gumagamit ang system ng US FHWA SSAM methodology para sa visual conflict detection:
- Time-to-Collision (TTC): Oras bago magbanggaan ang dalawang sasakyan kung 
  mananatili ang kanilang bilis at trajectory (Critical kung TTC < 1.5s).
- Post-Encroachment Time (PET): Oras sa pagitan ng pag-alis ng unang sasakyan sa 
  conflict point at pagdating ng kasunod na sasakyan (Critical kung PET < 1.2s).
- Conflict Classifications:
  * Rear-End Conflict (Dilaw na 3D pulse)
  * Crossing Conflict (Pulang 3D pulse)
  * Lane-Change / Merging Conflict (Kahel na 3D pulse)


2.7 DECISION SUPPORT SYSTEM & METRICS (metrics.ts & ComparisonCards.tsx)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Level of Service (LOS A hanggang F) base sa average delay per vehicle:
  * LOS A: Delay <= 10s (Free flow)
  * LOS B: Delay 10 - 20s
  * LOS C: Delay 20 - 35s
  * LOS D: Delay 35 - 55s
  * LOS E: Delay 55 - 80s
  * LOS F: Delay > 80s (Severe Congestion / Breakdown)
- Vehicle Throughput (vehicles per hour)
- Average Travel Speed (km/h)
- Total Simulated Conflicts (Rear-End, Crossing, Lane-Change)
- Scenario Comparison: Real-time side-by-side comparison ng "Existing Condition" 
  laban sa "Proposed Mitigation" (e.g. Signalized intersection, Pedestrian 
  Overpass, o Dedicated Bus Loading Bays).

4. COMPREHENSIVE RECOMMENDATIONS (KUNG PAANO AAYUSIN ANG LAHAT)
================================================================================

Narito ang step-by-step roadmap upang maging perpekto at 100% defense-ready 
ang inyong software:

1. KONTROLADONG BUS DISPATCH RATE (Realistic Scheduling):
   - Sa totoong buhay, hindi bawat 10 segundo dumarating ang Ceres bus.
   - REKOMENDASYON: Maglagay ng simpleng slider o dropdown sa Control Panel:
     "Bus Arrival Frequency: [1 bus every 30 mins / 1 hour / 2 hours]" 
     (naka-scale sa simulation minutes). Kapag naka-set sa "Realistic", may 
     oras kung kailan walang bus, at may peak hour kung kailan sunod-sunod ang 
     dating. Ito ang hinahanap ng mga civil engineering panelists.

2. PRE-SET SCENARIOS PARA SA DIRECT COMPARISON:
   - Gumawa ng tatlong (3) distinct selectable scenarios sa dashboard:
     * SCENARIO 0 (Baseline / Existing): Normal na gulo sa Guanzon St., 
       tricycle jaywalking, buses turning wide across two lanes, LOS E/F.
     * SCENARIO 1 (Traffic Light Intervention): Smart signalization sa Guanzon 
       at Bacolod-South Rd intersection.
     * SCENARIO 2 (Geometric & Terminal Improvement): Dedicated right-turn slip 
       lane para sa Ceres terminal, pedestrian barriers sa sidewalk, at 
       designated loading bay para sa tricycles.
   - Kapag pinindot ng panel ang "Compare", agad nilang makikita ang pagbaba ng 
     delay mula halimbawa 68s (LOS E) pababa ng 24s (LOS C).

3. CALIBRATION GAMIT ANG ACTUAL DATA (KABANKALAN LGU / PNP TRAFFIC DATA):
   - Maglagay ng "Volume Calibration Preset" kung saan pwede i-input ang 
     peak-hour traffic counts (e.g. Ceres buses: 18 veh/hr; Jeepneys: 45 veh/hr; 
     Tricycles: 120 veh/hr; Cars: 85 veh/hr).
   - Kapag naitanong ng panel: "Saan niyo nakuha ang numbers na yan?", pwede 
     sabihing naka-calibrate ito sa aktwal na ocular count o LGU traffic data.

4. USER INTERFACE & PRESENTATION MODE PARA SA DEFENSE:
   - Maglagay ng "Defense Presentation Mode" button na nagtatago ng 
     kumplikadong controls at nagpapakita ng malinis na camera angles:
     * Camera 1: Bird's Eye View (Top-Down ng buong network)
     * Camera 2: Ceres Terminal Gate 1 & 2 Focus (Ingress/Egress)
     * Camera 3: Guanzon St. Intersection Focus (Conflict hotspot)
     * Camera 4: KCC Mall & Market Frontage
   - Ito ay magbibigay ng "WOW factor" sa panelists habang nagpe-present.

5. FINAL BUG AUDIT & STABILITY GUARANTEE:
   - Panatilihin ang zero-tolerance sa vehicle overlapping at teleportation. 
   - Siguraduhin na kahit i-leave ang simulation nang tumatakbo ng 30 minuto, 
     hindi ito magka-crash o mag-i-stack ng sasakyan sa exit nodes.
